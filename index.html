Theory meets experiment.

EN

Our research group operates at the intersection of Systems & Synthetic Biology, Quantitative Biology, and Computational Biology. Our vision is to establish quantitative design principles and automated engineering platforms to program biological systems, addressing challenges in health and sustainability.

Our core strategy is to develop automated, model-guided platforms that enable the precise and predictable design of genetic components and circuits. By applying quantitative engineering methods, we assemble these modules into complex biological systems and microbial consortia with applications ranging from fundamental discovery to practical solutions.

We are a highly interdisciplinary team passionate about merging theory and experiment to push the boundaries of what is possible in engineering biology.

中文

本课题组的研究聚焦于系统与合成生物学、定量生物学及计算生物学的交叉领域。我们的目标是建立定量的设计原理与自动化的工程平台，以实现对生物系统的编程，从而解决科学和应用问题。

我们的核心策略是开发由计算模型指导的自动化平台，以实现对基因元件和功能线路的精准、可预测设计。我们运用定量化的工程方法，将这些模块组装成复杂的生物系统，以应用于基础科学探索与解决实际问题。

我们是一个高度交叉学科的团队，热衷于融合理论与实验，共同挑战并拓展工程生物学的边界。
