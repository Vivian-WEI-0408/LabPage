PROFILE

Engineering biology
with quantitative precision.

Dr. Ye Chen is a Principal Investigator at the Shenzhen Institutes of Advanced Technology (SIAT). He received his B.S. from Zhejiang University, his Ph.D. from Rice University with Prof. Matthew Bennett, and was a postdoctoral researcher at MIT with Prof. Christopher Voigt.

His research has been published in leading journals such as Science, Nature Microbiology, Nature Communications, and Nature Chemical Biology. The Chen Lab focuses on developing automated strategies and quantitative methods for the precise and predictable design of genetic circuits and complex biological systems.
